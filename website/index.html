Built for Those Who Value Privacy & Performance

Most PDF tools today either force recurring cloud subscriptions or upload your confidential files to remote servers. dpdf takes the opposite path.

100% Offline by Default

dpdf makes zero background calls, transmits zero telemetry, and doesn't ping servers on startup. Any update check requires your explicit, interactive consent first.

Native Rust Performance

No bloated Electron wrappers. Built natively in 100% safe Rust with GPU hardware-acceleration (wgpu/Vulkan/Metal/DirectX). Starts in milliseconds and handles gigabyte PDFs with ease.

Fearless Atomic Saves

Never worry about corrupted files. Edits are appended atomically to preserve original binary streams. Deep undo/redo stacks allow you to step back through any sequence of operations.

Portable & Dual-Scope

Install without administrative privileges into user profile, deploy system-wide via Enterprise WiX MSI, or run the Portable Edition directly from a flash drive with zero registry footprint.

All The Essential Tools, Zero Distraction

Everything you need for everyday and professional PDF document handling.

Visual Thumbnail Grid with Drag & Drop

Forms & Markup

Fill, Sign & Annotate with Ease

Interact with standard AcroForm text fields, checkboxes, and radio buttons. Add highlights, strikethroughs, shapes, and fill & sign dates with clean typography.

Document Assembly

Combine, Split & Protect with Security

Merge dozens of PDFs while retaining their outline bookmark hierarchies. Split giant documents by size or page counts. Add standard AES-128 or AES-256 password encryption with permission locks.

  • Split by page count, bookmarks, or chapter ranges
  • AES-256 and RC4 encryption with permission controls
